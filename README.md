How'd I get here?
-----------------
Curiosity was always the starting point.
It began with experimenting on a TI-84 with basic programming and taking apart my Nintendo 3DS, 
which grew into a fascination with how technology can be built, understood, and improved. 
Software development taught me how to create; data science taught me how to find meaning in complexity; 
and cybersecurity taught me the importance of protecting it.
Together, they’ve shaped my path toward becoming someone who can understand technology from the inside out, 
recognize what doesn’t belong, and help build a digital world that is thoughtful, secure, and resilient.
